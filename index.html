ScopeGuard

Fixed-price projects do not blow up all at once - they die one "quick favor" at a time. ScopeGuard logs every out-of-scope request, prices it at your real rate, and drafts the change-order email so you bill the creep instead of absorbing it.

Open ScopeGuard
  • Creep meter - total unbilled extras as a percent of the fixed price.
  • Status at a glance - on scope, creeping, or blown.
  • Change orders, written - one tap copies a polite per-request change-order email.
  • Private by design - everything stays in your browser.
